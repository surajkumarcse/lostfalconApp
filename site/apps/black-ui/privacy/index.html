Black UI privacy policy.

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Black UI is an Android launcher developed by Lost Falcon (LostFalcon), with application ID app.lostfalcon. This policy describes its current development implementation and related support. Black UI is not yet available to download.

The short version

Black UI does not require an account. Its launcher functionality processes information on your device and does not send your app list or launcher preferences to Lost Falcon. The current implementation has no advertising or analytics SDKs, developer-operated cloud sync, or direct internet access. Lost Falcon does not sell your app list or launcher preferences.

Installed-app information

To display and open your apps, Black UI reads launchable app names, package identifiers, launcher activity identifiers, and Android profile information through Android's launcher APIs. This can include work-profile apps when Android and your organization's policies allow access. Installed-app information is used for the home screen, app drawer, local search, and app actions; it is not uploaded to Lost Falcon.

Searching the app drawer filters this list on your device. Black UI does not store a history of those searches or a log of apps you open. It does not read other apps' private content.

Preferences stored on your device

Black UI saves your favorite-app selections, hidden-app selections, and custom app names in private app storage. Saved selections use package identifiers together with Android profile identifiers so personal and work apps can be distinguished. These preferences are used to restore your launcher layout.

Hiding an app removes it from Black UI's normal app list and home screen; it does not uninstall, lock, or hide it from Android or other apps. Renaming an app changes only its label in Black UI.

Retention and deletion

Saved preferences remain on the device until changed or removed. You can unhide apps, remove favorites, or restore original names within Black UI. Clearing Black UI's storage in Android settings or uninstalling it removes its locally stored preferences. This does not delete data held by other installed apps.

Android backup and device transfer

Black UI allows Android-managed backup. Depending on your device, operating system, account, and settings, Android may back up or transfer your saved preferences. Lost Falcon does not receive or access those backups. Clearing local storage does not necessarily remove an existing platform backup, and preferences may be restored during installation or device transfer. Manage backups through your device and account controls.

See Android's app backup documentation for platform details.

Permissions and actions you initiate

Black UI declares Android's permission to request app uninstallation. Choosing Uninstall opens Android's confirmation flow; Black UI does not silently delete apps. App info opens Android's settings for the selected app.

The phone and camera shortcuts open your dialer and camera apps. Black UI itself does not request access to contacts, call logs, SMS, location, camera, or microphone, and does not directly place calls or capture photos.

Swiping up opens a web-search handler, or Google in your browser if no search handler is available. Black UI does not maintain web-search history. Information you enter in a search app or browser is handled by that service under its own policies. Launching any other app similarly makes that app's data practices applicable.

If you contact support

Email lostfalconofficial@gmail.com for privacy questions or support. Lost Falcon receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send through the email provider, and uses them to respond and investigate your request.

Support correspondence is retained as needed for the request and related follow-up, subject to applicable obligations. You may email the same address to request deletion. Avoid sending passwords, sensitive information, or unrelated private data.

This policy website

Visiting this page is separate from using the launcher. The static Lost Falcon website has no account registration, advertising scripts, analytics scripts, or application-set tracking cookies. Firebase Hosting processes technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the website. Website hosting does not add Firebase SDKs to Black UI.

See the Lost Falcon website privacy policy, Firebase privacy information, and Google's privacy policy for more information.

Children and policy changes

Black UI does not ask users to submit personal information through its launcher interface. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support email, contact Lost Falcon to request review and deletion.

This policy will be updated if the app's data handling changes. The date above identifies the latest revision. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to lostfalconofficial@gmail.com.
