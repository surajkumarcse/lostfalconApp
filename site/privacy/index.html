Privacy policy.

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains information handled by the Tap & Count Android app, the Lost Falcon website, and support email.

The Android app

Tap & Count does not require an account. The app does not send your counts to Lost Falcon, and does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs in the reviewed implementation. Lost Falcon does not collect personal information through the app's counting functionality.

Local count history

When you reset a nonzero count, the app stores the completed total, its date, and a generated session identifier in a local database on your device. This allows you to view completed totals in History. A session identifier identifies a saved counting session; it is not an account identifier.

This local storage is distinct from collecting information on a developer-operated server. Clearing the app's storage through Android settings or uninstalling the app can remove locally stored information.

Android backup and device transfer

The app allows Android-managed backup. Depending on your device, operating system, account, and settings, Android may back up or transfer the app's local data, including saved history. This is a platform service, not a Lost Falcon cloud-sync service. Lost Falcon does not receive or access those backups. Manage backup and device-transfer settings through your device and account controls.

See Android's explanation of app backup for platform details.

Permissions

The app declares vibration permission to provide feedback when counting, and notification permission for counting-related notifications. Notification availability depends on your Android version and permission settings. The app's counting functionality does not request access to contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

If you contact support

If you email lostfalconofficial@gmail.com, your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send are received by Lost Falcon through the email provider. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate the issue you report.

Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, subject to applicable obligations. You may contact the same address to request deletion of your support correspondence. Do not include passwords, sensitive personal information, or unrelated private data in a support message.

The website

This static website has no account registration, contact form, advertising scripts, analytics scripts, or application-set tracking cookies. Fonts and app screenshots are served locally rather than embedded from third-party services.

When deployed on Cloudflare Pages, the hosting provider processes technical request information, such as IP address, requested URL, and browser information, to deliver and protect the website. Hosting infrastructure may process operational or security logs under its own policies. This is different from the Android app's local counting data.

See Cloudflare's privacy policy for information about the hosting provider's processing.

External services and links

Opening a Google Play link takes you to a Google service, governed by Google's policies. Sending email uses your email application and email providers. These services may process information independently of the Tap & Count app and this website.

Children's information

The app's counting functionality does not ask users of any age to provide personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information through support email, contact Lost Falcon so the message can be reviewed and appropriate deletion requested.

Policy changes and questions

This page will be updated if app behavior or website services change. The date above identifies the latest revision. For privacy questions or requests, contact lostfalconofficial@gmail.com.
