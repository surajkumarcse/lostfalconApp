A digital alternative to a handheld tally clicker, with controls that stay out of the way.

01 / TAP

A big target for little moments.

Tap the large counting area to add one. The number stays easy to read, so you can stay focused on what you are counting.

02 / BUTTONS

Count without looking down.

With the app open, press volume up to add one or volume down to subtract one. Use the controls that feel right for the task.

03 / UNDO

An extra tap? No problem.

Use Undo to subtract one after an accidental tap. Sound and vibration feedback help you notice each count.

04 / HISTORY

Finish a count. Start another.

Reset a nonzero count to save the completed total in local history and return to zero. Open History to revisit your saved totals.

WHAT WILL YOU COUNT?

A little help for
whatever adds up.

Exercise repetitions

Tap after each rep, or use the volume button when it is easier to reach. Reset between sets to keep a history of finished totals.

Knitting & crochet rows

Count each completed row once. If you undo a row in your project, subtract one in the app too. Keep pattern notes separately.

People & attendance

Add one for each person arriving. Use Undo to correct a duplicate tap. The app tracks a total, not names or attendee details.

Inventory & everyday tasks

Count one group of items at a time, then reset before starting the next. Keep item names separately; saved history contains totals and dates.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Can I use Tap & Count offline?

Yes. Counting and locally stored completed history do not require an internet connection. Downloading or updating the app through Google Play does.

Do I need an account?

No. You can open the app and start counting without signing up or logging in.

How do I save a count?

Choose Reset from the menu when the total is greater than zero. The app saves that completed total in History, then starts again at zero. Do not rely on an unfinished total surviving an app restart.

Is there an iPhone or browser version?

This website introduces the Android app. It is not an online counter, and no iPhone version is offered here.

Where does my count history go?

Completed totals and their dates are stored in the app's local database. Android may back up app data depending on your device settings. Lost Falcon does not operate a cloud-sync service for your counts. Read the privacy policy.
