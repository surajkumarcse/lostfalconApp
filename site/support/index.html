Getting started

  1. Install Tap & Count from Google Play and open it.
  2. Tap the large counting area to add one to the total.
  3. While the app is open, press volume up to add one or volume down to subtract one.
  4. Tap Undo to subtract one after an accidental tap. The counter does not go below zero.

The counting interaction provides sound and vibration feedback. Availability and perceived volume can depend on your device and system settings.

Saving a count and starting again

Open the menu near the top of the counting screen and choose Reset. If your count is greater than zero, the app saves the completed total and its date locally, then returns the counter to zero.

Choose History from the same menu to see your saved totals. History stores totals and dates, not named projects or item descriptions. Keep labels separately if you are counting multiple kinds of items.

Finish your session before closing the app. Use Reset to save a nonzero total; do not rely on an unfinished count surviving a restart. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app can remove local history. Android-managed backups may restore data depending on your device and backup settings, but they are not a guaranteed export or an app cloud-sync feature.

Common questions

Why do the volume buttons change the count?

The app uses these buttons as counting controls while it is open. Volume up adds one and volume down subtracts one. Leave the app or use your system volume controls when you want to adjust audio instead.

Why can't I subtract below zero?

The tally is a nonnegative total. Undo and volume down do not decrease a zero count.

Can I count without internet?

Yes. Counting and local completed history work offline. Google Play downloads, updates, and sending support email require connectivity.

Does it support named counters or cloud sync?

This site does not advertise named counters, cloud sync, widgets, or voice commands as available features. Use the app's current Google Play listing for release information.

How can I check device compatibility?

Open the Google Play listing on your Android device. Google Play will show whether the published app is available for that device.
